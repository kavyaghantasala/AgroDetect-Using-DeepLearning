How AgroDetect Works

📸

1. Capture or Upload

Take a clear, well-lit photo of an affected plant leaf and upload it in JPG or PNG format.

🧠

2. ResNet50 Neural Analysis

Our deep residual network extracts 2,048 multi-scale visual features and classifies leaf pathology.

🌾

3. Actionable Prevention

Receive immediate disease prognosis, confidence score, visible symptoms, and field-tested preventive measures.
